Assignment 1

Part A: Portfolio Analysis

Portfolio 1: Patrick's Engineering Portfolio

Navigability

Patrick's portfolio uses a sidebar that allows the user to access each assignment directly from the left side of the screen. The navigation is simple and consistent, allowing a reader to move between Patrick's work efficiently.

Reproducibility

Each assignment includes a description of the problem and the process used to reach the final result. The documentation includes calculations, resources, and supporting information used to develop the conclusions. This provides enough context for the reader to understand how the final result was reached rather than only presenting the answer.

Evidence of Reasoning

Patrick includes photographs, CAD drawings, calculations, and other supporting material throughout his assignments. These visuals are placed near the sections where they are discussed, connecting the evidence to the written explanation. This allows the reader to understand how the documented work supports the conclusions instead of only seeing the final result.

Professional Tone

Patrick's portfolio presents his work using organized sections, technical descriptions, calculations, CAD drawings, and supporting resources. The consistent structure allows the information to be presented in a format appropriate for an engineering audience and makes the work easier for a potential employer or colleague to review.

Portfolio 2: Evan Hoerl Engineering Portfolio

Portfolio 2: Evan Hoerl Engineering Portfolio

Navigability

Evan Hoerl's portfolio uses a simple navigation structure where the reader can scroll through a brief summary of his projects and select individual projects for more information. This allows a reader to quickly identify projects that are relevant to them without having to navigate through multiple pages.

Reproducibility & Evidence of Reasoning

The portfolio contains substantial visual documentation throughout the projects, which helps the reader understand the physical designs and results being presented. However, some project descriptions provide less information about how specific conclusions or design decisions were reached. The available documentation does not always explain the reasoning behind each decision in enough detail for another engineer to reproduce the process without additional information.

Professional Tone

The portfolio presents relevant engineering experience and provides contact information and a LinkedIn profile, giving a potential employer a direct way to learn more about Evan or contact him. The combination of project documentation, engineering experience, and professional contact information makes the portfolio appropriate for an engineering audience.

Overall Assessment

Evan's portfolio is effective at presenting engineering projects and professional experience through concise descriptions, visual evidence, and accessible project links. Its main limitation is that some projects could provide more documentation of the reasoning and decision-making process behind the final results.
